Title: Benefits Enrollment Guide
Category: HCM

Employees can update benefits during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event. Qualifying life events include marriage, birth or adoption of a child, change in employment status, or loss of other coverage.

Benefits updates are submitted through Employee Self-Service. The employee should open Benefits, select the active enrollment event, review available plans, attach any required documents, and submit the election.

After submission, employees can review the status in Employee Self-Service. A pending status means the benefits team has not completed review. An approved status means the election has been accepted.

If the enrollment event is missing, the employee should create an HR helpdesk ticket with the event type, event date, and any supporting documentation.

The helpdesk should not promise plan eligibility or coverage amounts unless the information is present in an approved benefits document.
