Title: VPN Reset and Remote Access Guide
Category: IT Support

Employees can reset a VPN profile from the Secure Access portal. Open the portal, select Reset VPN Profile, remove the old device binding, and restart the VPN client before signing in again.

If the VPN client asks for multi-factor authentication, approve the prompt from the registered authenticator app. If no prompt appears, verify that the device time is synchronized and that push notifications are enabled.

If the VPN client reports a certificate mismatch, refresh the device certificate from endpoint management and wait five minutes before reconnecting.

If VPN access still fails after these steps, create a helpdesk ticket with the employee email address, device name, VPN error message, and the time the issue occurred.

Laptop replacement is not handled through the VPN reset process. For hardware issues, the employee should submit a separate hardware support request.
