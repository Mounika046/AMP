Title: Day One Onboarding Checklist
Category: Onboarding

The day-one onboarding checklist includes laptop delivery, corporate email activation, badge request, payroll profile setup, benefits task review, and required training assignments.

Before the employee start date, the hiring manager should confirm the start date, shipping address, role, department, and required applications.

On the first day, the employee should sign in to corporate email, complete identity verification, enroll in multi-factor authentication, review the onboarding portal, and confirm access to required collaboration tools.

If the laptop has not arrived, the manager should create an onboarding support ticket with the employee name, start date, shipping address, and asset request number.

Required training should be completed within the first week unless a department-specific policy states a shorter deadline.
