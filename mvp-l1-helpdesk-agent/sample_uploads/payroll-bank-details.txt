Title: Payroll Bank Details FAQ
Category: Payroll

Employees can update payroll bank details in Employee Self-Service under Payroll and Payment Methods. The update should be submitted before payroll lock to apply to the next pay cycle.

Payroll lock usually occurs before payroll processing starts. Changes made after payroll lock may apply to the following pay cycle instead of the current one.

To update payment details, open Employee Self-Service, go to Payroll, choose Payment Methods, edit the bank account, verify routing details, and save the change.

For security reasons, payroll support cannot update bank details on behalf of an employee through chat. The employee must complete the update in Employee Self-Service.

If the employee entered incorrect bank information, they should create a payroll ticket with the pay period, payment method, and a description of the issue. They should not include full bank account numbers in the ticket description.
